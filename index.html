Miraculous Moments · Khoảnh Khắc Diệu Kỳ

A story before the film.

Miraculous Moments began with a volunteering experience where I met pediatric cancer survivors, listened to their stories, and shared moments of creativity and joy. Through photography, the children were not simply subjects of a story — they were making images of their own.

Before seeing their illness, I wanted people to see the children.

That experience later shaped The Window Without a Horizon and made me think more seriously about the responsibility of communication: not only how we tell a story, but how we choose to see the person inside it.

Chạm Việt Phục · Workshop & exhibition

Bringing heritage closer.

A student-led cultural project inviting people to engage with Vietnamese traditional clothing through display, conversation, and participation.

Culture becomes more meaningful when people can step into it.

What mattered most was creating a space where people could ask, listen, and participate — not simply look at cultural artifacts from a distance.

07
Tiên Thường Tuần Du · Cultural participation

Tradition in motion.

A cultural space where costume, performance, and community participation came together. I joined as a volunteer and experienced heritage as something carried by people, not kept behind glass.

Heritage becomes stronger when it is practiced together.

These events showed me how cultural projects can become spaces for participation, conversation, and connection across identities and traditions.
