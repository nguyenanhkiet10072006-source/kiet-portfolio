02 / Miraculous Moments

A day that changed
the way I looked.

A photography session with 13 pediatric cancer survivors completing their graduation projects.

Photography was the activity.
The real memory was the time we shared.

Between photo setups, we played games and talked about their dreams. Those ordinary moments helped me see the children beyond illness — something that later shaped the way I approached my screenplay.
